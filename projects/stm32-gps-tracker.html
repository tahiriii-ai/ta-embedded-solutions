01The challenge

An STM32, a GPS module and an SD card share one board, and each needs clean signals: the MCU's digital lines, the GPS RF front end and the SD data bus.

02My role

  • Board design and layout in KiCad
  • Ground plane and power management circuitry
  • GPS antenna keep-out and mounting holes for an enclosure
  • DRC/ERC verification

03Engineering approach

  • Dedicated ground plane and power management to keep each section quiet.
  • GPS module placed inside its own keep-out zone, clear of copper pour and crossing traces so reception is not compromised.
  • Multi-layer routing with a solid ground plane.

04PCB design

3D board

05Verification

DRC and ERC were run and reviewed before fabrication.

06Final output

  • KiCad design verified for fabrication

07Project result

A compact tracker board combining MCU, GPS and SD logging, with the GPS section isolated by a dedicated keep-out.
