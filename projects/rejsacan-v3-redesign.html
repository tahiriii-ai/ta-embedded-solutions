RejsaCAN v3.4 — PCB Redesign

01The challenge

A Fiverr client asked for a production-ready PCB layout and routing in EasyEDA Pro around the open-source RejsaCAN v3.4 schematic: an ESP32-S3 CAN bus node with USB-C, microSD logging and a switched-mode power stage.

The board had to hold roughly 80 components on a 30 × 50 mm footprint, which made it as much a placement problem as a routing job.

02My role

  • Component placement for the full 80-part design
  • Layout and routing of the board in EasyEDA Pro
  • Power-stage layout with thermal relief
  • Preparation for fabrication through JLCPCB

The schematic is the open-source RejsaCAN v3.4 design; the work was the PCB redesign.

03Engineering approach

The CAN transceiver, buck regulator, ESD protection network, SD card, JTAG header and full GPIO breakout all had to fit without compromising signal integrity or thermal relief on the power section.

  • ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 (N16R8) with USB Type-C and ESD-protected data lines
  • SN65HVD230DR CAN transceiver with onboard 120 Ω termination
  • LMR14006XDDCR buck converter feeding a switched-load LDO (MT9700)
  • PTC resettable fuse and TVS / flyback diodes on the power input
  • JTAG header alongside I2C, SPI and GPIO breakouts

05Verification

06Final output

  • Board ordered and fabricated through JLCPCB

07Project result

The client's schematic went the full distance to a fabricated board: about 80 components on a 30 × 50 mm PCB, ordered through JLCPCB.
