Water Level Indicator PCB

Sensor & Alert Board · 2-Layer · KiCad + Altium · KiCad, Altium Designer · Portfolio project

Tools
KiCad, Altium Designer
Layers
2
Sensing
4× BC547 stages
Input
4-pin screw terminal
Indicators
3 LEDs + buzzer
Supply
10 V rail
Date
March 2026

01The challenge

Four probe inputs, each driving a BC547 stage: as water reaches each probe its stage switches on, lighting a level LED, and the top stage also sounds a buzzer for a full-tank alert on a 10 V rail. The design was carried through both KiCad and Altium Designer.

02My role

  • Schematic capture, footprint assignment and layout
  • Reconciling footprint libraries and routing engines between KiCad and Altium
  • 3D visualization and DRC in both tools

03Engineering approach

  • Four BC547 level-detection stages fed from a 4-pin probe terminal.
  • Three LED level indicators plus a buzzer on the topmost stage.
  • Routing conflicts and footprint mismatches between the two toolchains resolved on the same schematic.

05Verification

DRC was cleared in both KiCad and Altium Designer.

06Final output

  • Verified layout in both KiCad and Altium Designer

07Project result

The same design carried cleanly through two EDA toolchains, with footprint and routing differences resolved.
